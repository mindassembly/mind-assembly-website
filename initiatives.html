01 / Flagship initiative

Personality exploration.

Selected as an initial project in October 2026. The experience is hosted externally and designed for reflection rather than diagnosis.

First selected initiative · external link

Think about patterns, not labels.

Explore a self-discovery tool that invites you to reflect on your preferences and tendencies. Treat its output as a starting point for curiosity, not as evidence of a psychological condition, ability or fixed personality type.
