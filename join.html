Get involved

Bring a question to the table.

Mind Assembly is a student-led psychology collective. We read research, ask what the evidence can tell us, and learn by talking things through. Start with a paper, an idea, or something you’ve been wondering about.

Find your starting point ↓

How would you like to take part?

Follow along, bring something to discuss, or tell us what you’d like to work on together.

Join the community

Meet others interested in psychology and follow the conversation in our WhatsApp group. You’re welcome to read along and introduce yourself when you’re ready. Other group members may be able to see your phone number.

Open the WhatsApp invite ↗

Write with us

Have a psychology question you’d like to explain or a paper you’d like to unpack? Send a short outline and, if you have one, a source you’d start with.

Pitch a piece ↗

Collaborate on research

Tell us what you’re investigating and what working together might look like. A research question and a few lines about the kind of input you’re seeking are enough to start.

Discuss a collaboration ↗

Discuss a reading

Pick a piece from the library and bring one question to the WhatsApp group. You might ask how a study reached its conclusion or what you’d want to know next.

Find a reading in Explore → Open the discussion group ↗

Share an idea

Suggest a topic, a resource, or a question we could explore together. Tell us what caught your attention and why you’d like to see it discussed.
