02 / Where you might contribute

No single kind of contribution defines this community.

Reading and questioning

Bring papers, analyse methods and introduce perspectives that deepen a discussion.

Writing and communication

Help turn well-supported psychological questions into useful, accurate content.

Planning and coordination

Contribute to organisation, outreach, design or event planning as projects become concrete.

03 / What to expect

An invitation, not a promise of credentials.

Mind Assembly is at an early stage. Opportunities will depend on team capacity, the nature of each project and agreed responsibilities.

Membership does not guarantee publication, authorship, internships, certificates or clinical training. We do not provide mental-health diagnosis or treatment through the community.

External services such as WhatsApp and Google Forms have their own privacy terms. Review the information they display before sharing personal details.
