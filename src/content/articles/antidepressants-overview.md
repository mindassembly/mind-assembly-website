## A class is not a complete treatment story

Antidepressants include several medication classes. Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) alter serotonin signalling by inhibiting its reuptake, but this pharmacological description does not by itself explain every clinical effect or predict whether a particular person will respond.

## What matters beyond the mechanism

Clinical decisions consider the symptoms being treated, the strength and limitations of evidence, contraindications, interactions, adverse effects, past treatment history, and patient preferences. Treatment response and side effects vary.

## Why timelines are important

Medication effects on neurotransmitter transporters can occur sooner than the changes in symptoms a patient may notice. It is therefore misleading to assume that knowing the immediate target tells us the complete clinical timeline.

## Reading research critically

When comparing trials, check the population, comparator, follow-up duration, outcome measures, dropout rates, and adverse-event reporting. A difference between group averages does not guarantee the same benefit for every individual.

## Safety note

This article is educational, not treatment advice. Medication should be initiated, changed or discontinued only with appropriate medical guidance. For personal decisions, consult a qualified clinician.
