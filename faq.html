Frequently asked questions

Start with the questions.

What Mind Assembly is, what it is not, how to participate and how to approach the personality exploration responsibly.

01 / Answers

A little more clarity.

We describe what we know today and avoid presenting ideas under consideration as established opportunities.

What is Mind Assembly?

Mind Assembly is a developing, student-led psychology community focused on research-informed discussion, intellectual curiosity, shared learning and collaboration.

Who can participate?

The community is intended for psychology students, graduates, early-career thinkers and others with a thoughtful interest in psychological science. Some future initiatives may have specific requirements that will be explained if they are launched.

Is Mind Assembly formally registered or affiliated with a university?

The available project record describes Mind Assembly as an independent initiative in its formative stage. This website does not claim formal registration, professional accreditation or university affiliation.

Are all the projects on the website already active?

No. Personality exploration was selected as the first project. Topics such as body image, emotional signalling and collaborative research have been discussed, but the latest meeting record did not finalise a formal study. Future journal clubs or events are ideas, not confirmed activities.

What is the personality test, and can it diagnose me?

No. This is not an official or standardized psychometric test. This tool is created for research and self-discovery purposes only and should not be considered as a clinical or professional diagnosis. It is hosted on a separate website with its own data practices.

Is joining the WhatsApp group the same as becoming a founding-team member?

No. The group is a place for community connection. Specific organising responsibilities or project roles depend on mutual agreement and the needs of the activity.

Will I receive a certificate, internship or research authorship?

Participation does not guarantee credentials, certificates, internships, authorship or publication. Research credit and opportunities depend on actual contributions, applicable standards and project-specific arrangements.

Does Mind Assembly offer therapy or psychological assessments?

No. Mind Assembly is a peer learning and discussion community, not a provider of clinical services, professional diagnosis or individual treatment.

Can I send an article or research question for discussion?

Yes. Email mindassembly2026@gmail.com with a paper citation or a concise question and why you think it is worth discussing. Submission does not guarantee publication or selection.

How do I ask for my name to be removed from the People page?

Email mindassembly2026@gmail.com with the name to correct or remove. We will use that request to update the public directory.

How do I contact Mind Assembly?

Email mindassembly2026@gmail.com. You can also use the linked Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp channels for community contact.
