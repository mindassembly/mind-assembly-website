THE PEOPLE / MIND ASSEMBLY

The people behind the questions.

Mind Assembly begins with curiosity. Different people bring different questions, experiences and ways of looking at psychology. What brings us together is the willingness to listen, question and learn from one another.

Meet the collective ↓
A COLLECTIVE IN PROGRESS

Not everyone has to think alike.

A good conversation rarely begins with everyone agreeing. It begins when someone asks a question, someone else sees it differently, and both are willing to explore what follows.

We're building a space where reading, research, discussion and creative thinking can meet. Each person contributes to that process in their own way.

An assembly is made by the people who take part in it.
