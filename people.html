02 / Contributing together

Many kinds of contribution matter.

A psychology community needs researchers, readers, writers, thoughtful critics, people who organise meetings, and people who know how to turn ideas into accessible communication.

We are developing responsibilities and ways of working as the community grows. People do not have to publish research or hold a formal title to participate meaningfully.
