02 / A note on privacy

Know where your link leads.

Our homepage and information pages do not ask you to create an account. When you click on WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Forms or the personality exploration, you will be directed to a third-party website and its respective terms and privacy practices.

Do not share sensitive mental-health details in a public group or general enquiry message. Mind Assembly does not offer emergency response or professional mental-health care.

To request an update or removal from the public list of consenting names, email mindassembly2026@gmail.com.
